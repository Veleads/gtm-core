# Personas: Northwind Ledger

## finance-leader (decision maker)
- **Cares about:** close speed, headcount, audit risk, cash visibility.
- **Pains in their words:** "We close on day 9 and I want day 5." "I can't add heads this year."
- **What they ignore:** feature lists, "AI-powered", anything that sounds like a big ERP project.
- **Proof that lands:** peer manufacturer cut close by days; payback period.

## ap-owner (champion)
- **Cares about:** invoice backlog, supplier chasers, approval bottlenecks, their team's overtime.
- **Pains in their words:** "Approvers sit on invoices for weeks." "We re-key every invoice."
- **What they ignore:** strategic finance transformation talk.
- **Proof that lands:** invoices processed per FTE, time to approve.
