# ICP: Northwind Ledger

> Human-readable rationale. The machine-readable version is `icp.yaml`. Keep them in sync.
> Approved by Maria Chen on 2026-09-12 (recorded in icp.yaml).

## Who buys
Mid-market companies with physical supply chains (manufacturing, wholesale, distribution) whose
finance team processes 1,000+ supplier invoices a month and still keys them in manually or in a
legacy ERP add-on.

## Segments
1. **uk-manufacturing-mm**: UK manufacturers, 200–1,500 employees. Best win rate (38%) in closed-won history.
2. **dach-wholesale-mm**: DACH wholesale/distribution, 200–1,000 employees. Fastest cycles; German-language copy needed later.

## Personas
- **Finance leader (decision maker):** CFO, Finance Director, VP Finance.
- **AP / finance ops owner (champion):** Head of AP, Financial Controller, Finance Operations Manager.

## Why they buy (from won deals)
- Month-end close slips because invoices sit in inboxes.
- Headcount freeze but invoice volume growing.
- ERP migration forces a rethink of AP.

## Exclusions
- Companies under 150 employees (too small, churn).
- Public sector, financial services (different buying process).
- Competitors and existing customers (see suppression in campaign config).
